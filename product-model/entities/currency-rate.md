# Currency rate

Currency, date, conversion values and rate classification

## Information kept

- **Currency** — The monetary unit of the retained amount.
- **Date** — Date associated with the retained historical entry.
- **Conversion values** — Retained dated conversion values used by the relevant calculations.
- **Rate classification** — Configured conversion category used by the corresponding report or pricing calculation.
