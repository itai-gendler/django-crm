# Income snapshot

Captured HTML, capture time, ownership/modification and department

## Information kept

- **HTML** — Supplied or captured report markup retained as the snapshot.
- **Captured** — Timestamp of the retained snapshot capture.
- **Owner** — The account responsible for this record.
- **Modified by** — The account retained as the last modifying person.
- **Department** — The organizational scope retained on this record.
